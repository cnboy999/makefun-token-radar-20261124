Which pre-token protocol is worth farming next?

Every protocol that records no token is scored on its own figures — log-scaled TVL, 7-day growth, age with no token, chain breadth and a category prior — and ranked by the implied value of its launch. Exchanges, bridges and wrapped or staked products are excluded: their TVL is another token's value, not a launch. Implied value is this radar's claim, not a promise and not a price.

Say it as the data says it: a protocol records no token. That is not proof it has none.

How implied value is computed — radar-2

implied = round( TVL × 0.25 × likelihood × growthMult × maturityMult )

likelihood = 0.30·logTVL + 0.20·categoryPrior + 0.18·growth + 0.17·ageHump + 0.15·chainBreadth  ·  growthMult = 1 + clamp(7d, −60, +120) ÷ 100 × 0.5  ·  maturityMult = 0.55 + 0.45·ageHump

Size, with diminishing returns

TVL is log-scaled from the $5M floor to $10B. $100M to $1B matters far more than $5B to $6B.

Growth, clamped

Seven-day TVL change, clamped to −60%…+120%, so one wild week cannot crown a protocol.

Age hump, peak 300–700 days

Old enough to be real, young enough to still launch. Under 30 days is unproven; past 1,500 days with no token, the launch is less likely. Unknown age counts as 365 days.

Category prior

Dexs, Lending, Yield, Restaking, Derivatives, Prediction Markets, Risk Curators, Allocators and RWA score highest — user-facing apps with points programmes. Chains, payments and wrappers score lowest.

Chain breadth

log(1 + chains) rewards protocols live on several chains, capped so five chains is enough.

What is excluded, and why

CEX, Bridge, Canonical Bridge, Liquid Staking, Liquid Restaking and Restaked BTC, plus versioned and wrapped/staked names, are never ranked as early rows. The watchlist (40 rows) is always included regardless.

No protocol name is special-cased anywhere in the model — see isRankable, likelihoodOf and impliedValue in lib/model.ts. The same function scores every row; the farm plan is templated from category, chains, TVL and age.
